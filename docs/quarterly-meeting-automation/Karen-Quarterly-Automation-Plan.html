Quarterly Meeting Prep

Automation Plan for Client Review Process

Prepared June 2026  |  Confidential

What is this? A plan to automate the quarterly client meeting preparation process — the data gathering, tax calculations, scorecard building, and agenda writing that happens before every client meeting.

The goal: Reduce prep time from ~90 minutes per client down to ~30 minutes (a quick manual payroll download plus review), while keeping all the professional judgment and client relationships fully in your hands.

No new software needed. Everything is built using tools already in the tech stack.

2. Your Current 14 Steps (and What Changes)

Here are the 14 steps you currently do by hand for every quarterly meeting, and what happens to each one:

  • 1
    Check Karbon for pending items
    Now: You open Karbon, click through each work item. After: System auto-pulls all pending items and summarizes them for you.
  • 2
    Download payroll / pay stubs
    Stays manual for now. You (or an admin) log into the payroll software, download the pay stubs, and drop them in the client folder — just like today. We are intentionally not connecting directly to every client's payroll system. (This can be automated later if desired.)
  • 3
    Organize documents into client folders
    Now: You create folders, drag files. After: System auto-creates folders and files everything with standard naming.
  • 4
    Check QuickBooks bank feeds
    Now: You open QBO and review. After: System checks automatically and flags problems. You review the flags only.
  • 5
    Fix QuickBooks categorization rules
    Now: You hunt down and edit rules by hand in QuickBooks. After: The system runs its own rulebook — it categorizes incoming transactions on a schedule using rules we control, and suggests new rules when it spots recurring patterns. You just approve the rule changes.
  • 6
    Reclassify transactions
    Now: You (or Keto) reclassify transactions one by one. After: AI proposes the full reclassification list in a review sheet — you tick approve, and the system applies the changes to QuickBooks in bulk, with a complete audit log of what changed.
  • 7
    Download P&L and Balance Sheet
    Now: You run reports in QBO, set dates, export. After: System auto-pulls for the right date ranges and saves to Drive.
  • 8
    Calculate estimated tax payments
    Now: You copy a ProConnect return, plug in numbers, calculate. After: System auto-populates a tax calculator from the financials. You review and apply your judgment on projections.
  • 9
    Enter W-2 withholding from pay stubs
    Now: You read pay stubs and type numbers. After: Once you've dropped the pay stubs in the folder, AI reads them and fills the withholding numbers into the tax calculator for you. You review — no retyping. (No payroll connection needed.)
  • 10
    Model C-Corp tax savings
    Now: You duplicate the return, remove C-Corp, compare. After: System runs both scenarios automatically. You verify the result.
  • 11
    Research tax strategies
    Now: You research from scratch in Gemini and NotebookLM. After: Blue J — our purpose-built tax research AI — plus Claude produce a cited research memo tailored to the client's situation. You validate the conclusions; the professional judgment stays 100% yours.
  • 12
    Build client scorecard
    Now: You pull numbers from P&L and Balance Sheet, calculate trends. After: System calculates all 4 metrics and writes a brief explanation. You review.
  • 13
    Write meeting agenda
    Now: You write talking points from scratch. After: System drafts the full agenda from all collected data. You customize and add your personal insights.
  • 14
    Assign tasks to team members
    Now: You message Keto or create tasks manually. After: System auto-creates work items/tasks in Karbon with context.

6. The Tax Calculation Engine

The system builds a tax estimate automatically from the financial data it collects. Here's the logic it follows:

flowchart TD
    A["Financial Data\n(from QuickBooks)"] --> B["Project Annual Income\n= YTD ÷ months × 12"]

    C["Payroll Data\n(from manually\nuploaded pay stubs)"] --> D["Owner W-2 Salary\n+ Withholdings"]

    B --> E["Net Profit\n= Income − Expenses"]
    E --> F["Pass-Through to Owner\n= Net Profit × ownership %"]

    F --> G["Total Taxable Income\n= Pass-through + W-2 + Other"]
    D --> G

    G --> H["Federal Tax\n(marginal brackets)"]
    G --> I["State Tax\n(CA, NY, etc.)"]

    H --> J["Subtract\nWithholdings & Credits"]
    I --> J

    J --> K["Net Amount Due\n÷ by quarterly schedule"]

    K --> L["💰 Quarterly Payment\nAmounts for Client"]

    M{"Does client have\na C-Corp?"} -->|"Yes"| N["Run Scenario A:\nWith C-Corp\nvs.\nScenario B:\nWithout C-Corp"]
    N --> O["💡 Tax Savings\n= Difference"]

Your Judgment Still Matters

The system fills in the numbers, but you still review the projections. For example, when a client's income "started late" and you decide to use 50% instead of 40% as the projection factor — that's professional judgment the system can't replace. It flags these decisions for you instead of guessing.

More Built-In Conveniences

  • One-click approvals: The prep-ready email includes Approve / Request Changes buttons. Nothing client-facing goes out until you click Approve.
  • Automatic document chasing: Document requests go out as Karbon client requests, which remind the client automatically until they upload — no more manual follow-up emails.
  • Pre-meeting client questionnaire: A short 3-question form goes out with the meeting reminder ("any big purchases, new entities, questions?"). The answers feed straight into your agenda, so the meeting covers what the client actually cares about.
  • Payment reminders: Once you approve the quarterly estimates, the system schedules reminder emails to the client (and Christina) before each federal and state due date, with the exact amounts and payment links.
  • Prep status dashboard: A live dashboard (free, via Looker Studio) shows every client's prep status at a glance — what's collected, what's missing, what's flagged, and which meetings are coming up.

10. Implementation Timeline

The system will be built in phases over approximately 3 weeks, with testing in the final week:

Week 1
Foundation & Data Collection
Set up the Client Profile spreadsheet, email templates, and folder structure. Build the auto-pulls from QuickBooks and Xero, the Karbon pending-items pull, Karbon client document requests with auto-reminders, and the file intake detection. (Payroll stays a manual download for now.)
Week 2
Processing & Meeting Output
Build the tax estimation templates, entity comparison model, and scorecard generator with AI. The reclassification approve-and-apply engine and the categorization rules engine. Then the agenda generator (with Blue J research memos and questionnaire answers), presentation slides, Karbon task auto-creation, one-click approvals, payment reminders, notifications, and Fathom post-meeting processing.
Week 3
Testing & Go-Live
Test with real clients, fix any issues, documentation, training, and short support period.
