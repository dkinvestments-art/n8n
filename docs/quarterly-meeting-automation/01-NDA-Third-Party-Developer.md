## 1. PURPOSE

The Firm engages in tax preparation, accounting, and financial advisory services
for clients. The Firm desires to engage the Developer to build workflow
automation systems using n8n and related software tools. This Agreement governs
the confidentiality obligations of both parties in connection with that
engagement.

---

## 2. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

"Confidential Information" means any and all non-public information disclosed by
either party, whether orally, in writing, electronically, or by inspection,
including but not limited to:

### 2.1 Firm Confidential Information
- (a) Internal business processes, workflows, standard operating procedures
- (b) Client lists, client names, and client relationship details
- (c) Software configurations, API architecture, and system integrations
- (d) Pricing structures, fee schedules, and financial information of the Firm
- (e) Business strategies, marketing plans, and growth plans
- (f) Employee and contractor information

### 2.2 Specially Protected Information (Tax Return Information)
The following categories are subject to heightened protections under
Section 7216 of the Internal Revenue Code and shall NOT be disclosed to the
Developer under any circumstances unless a separate 7216 Consent Addendum
(Exhibit A) is executed:

- (a) Any information furnished by a client in connection with the preparation
  of a tax return
- (b) Client Social Security Numbers, Employer Identification Numbers, or
  Individual Taxpayer Identification Numbers
- (c) Client income, deduction, or tax liability information
- (d) Client financial statements, profit and loss statements, or balance
  sheets containing actual client data
- (e) Tax returns, tax return drafts, workpapers, or tax planning documents
  containing client information
- (f) Any information that could identify a specific taxpayer and their
  financial position

### 2.3 Developer Confidential Information
- (a) Proprietary development methodologies and tools
- (b) Pricing and rate structures
- (c) Client lists of the Developer

---

## 3. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECEIVING PARTY

### 3.1 Non-Disclosure
The Receiving Party agrees to:
- (a) Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence
- (b) Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior
  written consent of the Disclosing Party
- (c) Not use Confidential Information for any purpose other than the
  authorized engagement
- (d) Limit internal disclosure to employees or contractors with a legitimate
  need to know, provided they are bound by confidentiality obligations at least
  as protective as this Agreement

### 3.2 No Subcontracting Without Consent
The Developer shall not engage subcontractors, freelancers, or other third
parties to perform any portion of the work without the Firm's prior written
consent. Any approved subcontractor must execute a confidentiality agreement
with protections equivalent to this Agreement.

### 3.3 Security Measures
The Receiving Party shall implement and maintain reasonable security measures
to protect Confidential Information, including but not limited to:
- (a) Encryption of data at rest and in transit
- (b) Access controls and authentication
- (c) Secure development environments
- (d) Regular deletion of unnecessary data

---

## 4. EXCLUSIONS

Confidential Information does not include information that:
- (a) Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party
- (b) Was already known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure
- (c) Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of
  Confidential Information
- (d) Is received from a third party not under a confidentiality obligation

**NOTE:** The exclusions in this Section 4 do NOT apply to Specially Protected
Information defined in Section 2.2. Tax return information is protected
regardless of how it is obtained under IRC Section 7216.

---

## 5. DATA HANDLING AND DESTRUCTION

### 5.1 Synthetic Data Requirement
The Developer shall use only synthetic, fabricated, or sandbox data for all
development and testing. The Developer acknowledges that this approach is
designed to avoid triggering disclosure obligations under IRC Section 7216.

### 5.2 Return and Destruction
Upon completion of the engagement or upon request by the Disclosing Party:
- (a) The Receiving Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information
  within ten (10) business days
- (b) The Receiving Party shall provide written certification of destruction
- (c) Destruction shall include secure deletion of electronic files,
  including backups

### 5.3 Retention Exception
The Receiving Party may retain one (1) archival copy of non-client
Confidential Information solely for legal compliance purposes, provided it
remains subject to this Agreement.

---

## 6. TERM AND SURVIVAL

### 6.1 Term
This Agreement shall remain in effect for the duration of the engagement and
for a period of five (5) years following the termination of the engagement.

### 6.2 Survival of Tax Data Protections
Obligations relating to Specially Protected Information (Section 2.2) shall
survive indefinitely and shall not expire.

---

## 7. REMEDIES

### 7.1 Injunctive Relief
Both parties acknowledge that a breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable
harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate. The non-breaching party
shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedies
available at law or in equity.

### 7.2 Indemnification
The Receiving Party shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Disclosing
Party from and against any and all claims, damages, losses, costs, and expenses
(including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from the Receiving Party's
breach of this Agreement.

### 7.3 Regulatory Consequences
The Developer acknowledges that unauthorized disclosure of tax return
information may constitute a criminal violation under IRC Section 7216,
punishable by fines and/or imprisonment, and may expose the Firm to regulatory
action by the IRS, state boards of accountancy, and other regulatory bodies.
