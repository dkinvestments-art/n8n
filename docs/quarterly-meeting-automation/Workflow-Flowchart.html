Step-by-Step: Who Does What

StepWhat HappensType
Calendar triggerSystem spots the quarterly meeting 24-48 hrs out and starts the prepAutomatic
Client profile lookupReads the client's setup to decide how to route everythingAutomatic
Karbon pending itemsPulls open work items and recent communicationsAutomatic
Financial data (with access)Auto-pulls P&L and Balance Sheet from QuickBooks / XeroAutomatic
Financial data (no access)Karbon sends the client a document request that auto-reminds until they uploadAutomatic
Client uploads docsClient drops financials into their secure folderClient
Parse uploaded docsAI reads whatever format arrives and structures the numbersAutomatic
Download pay stubsYou/an admin download pay stubs and drop them in the folder — no payroll logins on our sideManual
Read pay stubsAI reads the uploaded stubs and fills withholding into the calculatorAI + Review
Pre-meeting questionnaireSystem sends 3-5 questions; answers feed the agendaAutomatic Client
Bank feed health checkFlags sync issues and categorization problems for you to reviewAI + Review
Categorization rulesn8n's own rules engine categorizes transactions; AI suggests new rules, you approveAI + Review
Reclassify transactionsAI proposes the full list; you tick approve; system applies to QuickBooks in bulk with an audit logAI + Review
Tax estimationAuto-populates the calculator and runs projections; you review the judgment callsAI + Review
Entity comparisonRuns the with/without C-Corp scenarios; you verify assumptionsAI + Review
Tax strategy researchBlue J + Claude produce a cited memo; you validate every conclusionAI + Review
Client scorecardCalculates the 4 metrics and writes the narrative; you reviewAI + Review
Meeting agendaDrafts the full agenda from everything collected; you customizeAI + Review
Approval gateNothing client-facing goes out until you click ApproveGate
Generate docs / slidesBuilds the final agenda doc and presentationAutomatic
Create Karbon tasksAuto-creates work items for anything needing team actionAutomatic
Schedule payment remindersQueues reminders before each federal/state due date with amounts + linksAutomatic
Notify youSends the prep-ready email with linksAutomatic
Client meetingThe advisory conversation — fully yoursManual
Fathom transcriptRecords and transcribes the meetingAutomatic
Extract action itemsAI pulls action items from the transcriptAutomatic
Karbon tasks + notesCreates follow-up tasks and logs meeting notesAutomatic
Follow-up emailDrafts the client follow-up; you approve before it sendsAI + Review
Monitoring dashboardEvery run logs to a live dashboard showing status, gaps, and upcoming meetingsAutomatic
